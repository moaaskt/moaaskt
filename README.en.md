## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a software developer focused on **Backend Development**, with professional experience in **PHP, CodeIgniter 4, MySQL, REST APIs, and web applications in production**.

My experience includes developing and maintaining applications, integrating external services, implementing business logic, and investigating issues in real-world systems.

I also build independent projects using **Laravel, Node.js, NestJS, TypeScript, Go, and Python**, exploring SaaS architectures, automation, and artificial intelligence solutions.

I'm particularly interested in building well-structured, reliable, and maintainable systems. I enjoy understanding the problem before choosing the right technical solution.
